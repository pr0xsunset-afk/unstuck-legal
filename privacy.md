# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 10, 2026_

## Your data stays on your device

Unstuck processes what you say locally on your iPhone. The transcript is sent to Groq (our AI provider) only to generate your next step, then discarded. We don't store it anywhere.

## What we don't collect

No analytics. No tracking. No advertising identifiers. We don't know who you are or what you do outside this app.

## What we do store

Your settings (ambient sound, haptics) sync via Apple's iCloud. Your daily unblock count is stored device-local to enforce free-tier limits. That's it.

## Third-party services

- **Groq** — processes your unblock requests ([groq.com/privacy-policy](https://groq.com/privacy-policy))
- **Apple** — handles subscription billing and iCloud sync

## Your rights

Delete all stored data anytime: **Settings → Delete my data**.
Cancel your subscription anytime: **iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Unstuck**.
